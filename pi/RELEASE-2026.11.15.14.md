Builds on 2026.11.15.13 (/admin uses the Video Hub's own styles).

- The "Twitter" link (x.com/wokearewe1) is gone from the Video Hub's social
  links bar. It was the only Twitter/X link on the public pages, the lite site
  and /admin; there were no Twitter icons or share buttons.

  too. Links shared on any site still get the title, description and picture
  from the standard Open Graph tags, which are unchanged.

  Unedited) are centred with even spacing (18px apart, 4px between rows if
